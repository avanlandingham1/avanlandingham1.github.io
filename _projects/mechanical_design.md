## Motor-Generator Assembly

Each motor-generator pair is coupled mechanically usig shaft couplings to a torque transducer. Shaft misalignment introduces potentially harmful vibrations that can damage equipment and pose a safety risk to lab personnel.

## Safety Enclosure

A safety enclosure was designed and fabricated to allow safe operation of the electric machines. The primary structure is assembled from 1" aluminum extrusion using in-line and perpendicular pivots. 

Additional features:
* Impact-resistant polycarbonate
* Handles 
* Rubber bumbers
* Cable pass-throughts
