While at Boeing, I worked on a number of proprietary projects as a configuration design engineer. In this role I developed tools and conducted trade studies to analyze hybrid-electric aircraft concepts.

My responsibilities included: 
* Calibrating vehicle performance and weights to publicly available aircraft information
* Coordinating with subject matter experts to establish future technology levels and projections
* Manitaining operating cost models for evaluating configurations
* Executing trade studies to evaluate configuration impacts of electrification 
* Presenting results to leadership and executives

Size-classes considered: 
* Thin-haul commuter (e.g. PC-12)
* Large Part 23 (e.g. King Air)
* Part 23 Cargo (e.g. Cessna Sky Courier, Cessna Caravan)
* Regional Turboprop (e.g. DHC-8-400)
* Regional Jet (e.g. Embraer E175)
* Single-aisle
